Privacy Statement

XCA Ltd, doing business as Xities or Xities.com (“we” or “us”), values you as our customer and recognizes that privacy is important to all of us. This Privacy Statement explains how we collect, use and disclose personal data when you use our website and ride services, your rights in deciding what we do with the data we collect or hold about you, and how to contact us.

Privacy Statement Summary

What does this Privacy Statement cover?

It covers personal data we handle when you visit Xities.com, call us to book a ride, sign up for offers, or ride with a driver in our network of licensed independent operators in Colorado.

What personal data do we collect, and how?

  • Data you give us: your name, phone number, email address, pickup and drop-off locations, and trip date and time when you book, plus anything you share when you contact us or sign up for offers.
  • Data we collect automatically: basic device and browser information, your approximate location based on your internet connection (so we can confirm you are in our service area), and cookies or similar technology, with your consent where consent is required.
  • Data from others: information from our service providers, such as payment confirmations and trip status from drivers and dispatch.

How do we use it?

To quote, book, dispatch and complete your rides; to let you and your driver reach each other; to take payment; to send trip updates; to send offers if you asked for them; to prevent fraud; to meet our legal and regulatory obligations; and to improve our service.

When is your personal data shared?

We share personal data only as needed to provide your ride and run our business: with the licensed driver assigned to your trip; with service providers that act for us, such as payment processing, calls and text messaging, email, mapping and location services, and website hosting; and when the law requires it. Calls and texts between you and your driver go through a private relay number, so your personal phone number is not shown to the driver. We do not sell your personal data.

What are your rights and choices?

You can opt out of marketing emails at any time with the “unsubscribe” link in any marketing email, and opt out of texts by replying STOP. You can also ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Colorado residents have additional rights under the Colorado Privacy Act where it applies.

How to contact us

Questions about how we handle your personal data, or a request about your data? Call us at 833 950 1757.

XCA Ltd Policy Details

Cancellation Policy

  • Free cancellation window. You can typically cancel a ride request for free within a short window (usually 2 to 5 minutes) after the driver accepts, provided they have not yet arrived or aren't significantly close.
  • When fees apply. A cancellation charge is triggered if you cancel after the grace period, once the driver is on their way, or if the driver arrives and you do not show up (no-show).
  • Fee amounts. Premium ride types like SUVs and Black generally feature higher cancellation and no-show fees. The exact dollar amount varies by market, time, and distance traveled by the driver before the cancellation.
  • Scheduled rides. For pre-scheduled rides, you can cancel without penalty up to 60 minutes before the scheduled pickup time. Canceling within that 60-minute window results in a fee.
  • Weather, traffic and your confirmation email. Bad weather and traffic conditions affect the driver's ability to be on schedule. This policy applies in real time when you are booking. A disclosure in the email confirming your trip overrides any other web disclosure regarding this policy. Where no email disclosure is issued when confirming your booking, our standard cancellation policy applies.
